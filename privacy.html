Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Short Fuse ("the app") is a private tool operated by otiyyy (Royal Ascent Esports) that publishes our own short video clips to social media accounts we own or manage.

What we collect

  • When an account owner connects TikTok, TikTok gives the app an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through TikTok Login Kit, with the scopes user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload.
  • The app stores these tokens only on the operator's own computer, to post clips the owner approved. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than TikTok's own API.
  • The app does not collect information about viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish the approved video clips to the connected account and to check whether a post was published.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the app is removed. Revoke access any time in TikTok (Settings, Security, Manage app permissions); the app then can no longer post. To have locally stored data deleted, email us.
